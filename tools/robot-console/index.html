Posture

The robot settles out of standing on its own when idle. Gestures and walks are refused unless it is standing — tick auto stand-up below and the console will do it for you.

Gestures — all 10

Each is the same GesturePlayer sequence the keyboard teleop uses. welcome and greet are the same bow + shake-hand move.

Move

Speed is a fraction of the cap (0.6 m/s / 1.0 rad/s), so 1.0 means 0.6 m/s. Motion always auto-stops when the duration elapses.

Walk a measured distance

Closed-loop on odometry and accurate to a few cm, unlike the timed move above which undershoots. Blocks until the walk finishes — about 7 s for 30 cm.

Every tool the assistant invokes appears above as it is chosen, with the delay measured from the moment you stopped speaking — green is snappy, amber is noticeable, red feels broken. Analyse last call pulls Vapi's own server-side timings, which show where the time really went.
